# Paperly branding rules

Official product identity: **Paperly**. The uppercase wordmark **PAPERLY** is permitted. The canonical configuration is `packages/config/brand.json`; future UI, page metadata, email templates, PDF producer metadata and documentation generation must consume it rather than introduce alternate product names.

## Identity

Paperly helps people complete document tasks with clear controls and verifiable results. Voice: direct, calm and precise. Proposed tagline: “Your documents. Clearly handled.” It is a design proposal, not a performance guarantee.

Original logo direction: two offset sheets with a folded upper-right corner and an emerald stroke suggesting a P through negative space. Create an original SVG in Phase 1 and test it at favicon size, in monochrome and on dark/light backgrounds. No finished logo or trademark clearance is claimed in Phase 0.

Use emerald `#047857` for primary actions, dark ink `#0F172A`, white `#FFFFFF` and cool surface `#F8FAFC`. Validate contrast in the actual rendered UI; never encode status through color alone. Use a system sans-serif stack initially, 16px body text, visible keyboard focus, comfortable spacing and restrained borders/shadows. Any later fonts/icons/assets require licence records.

Customer-facing names, logo, URLs and wording must be original. Sejda is only an internal functional reference named in the project brief; do not use its branding, source, proprietary assets, pricing or copy, or imply affiliation. No reference-site content was copied during this audit.

Do not claim customer counts, certifications, perfect conversion, secure redaction, permanent deletion deadlines, subscription activation or production readiness without evidence. Whiteout must be labeled as a visual overlay that does not securely remove underlying content. Drawn signatures are not certificate-backed digital signatures.

Before release, review English and French copy, metadata, alt text, emails and generated file properties for consistent Paperly identity. Keep legal company identity, support email and domain unset until supplied. Name/domain/trademark availability has not been established.
