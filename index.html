YOUR FILES, YOUR SPACE

On your device. In your control.

This preview processes supported PDFs in browser memory. Your documents are not uploaded. Accounts, cloud storage and server conversions are not connected.

File handling details

A few things worth knowing.

Which tools can I use today?

Merge, extract pages and rotate produce real PDFs locally. The full directory previews the planned product scope; other tools are not yet available.

Does Paperly change my original file?

No. Local tools create a new download. Keep your original document, especially when it has signatures, forms or complex interactive elements.

Can I use Paperly offline?

This local preview bundles its PDF engine. A standalone desktop application has not been built, and no desktop download is available.
